### Hi 👋, I'm Minh

**Software Engineer · Computer Science PhD Student · Systems Tinkerer**

I like building things, taking systems apart to understand how they work, and occasionally turning small problems into unnecessarily elaborate side projects.

My research is mostly around **distributed systems, storage, key management, security, and performance**. Right now, I work on distributed KMS architectures and look at how they behave under load, scaling pressure, and failure.

Outside of research, things get a little less focused.

I spend a lot of time experimenting with **Linux, NixOS, self-hosting, local AI, developer tooling, infrastructure, automation**, and whatever technical rabbit hole seems interesting that week.

A surprisingly common pattern is:

> Find a tool → try it → find something annoying → try four alternatives → wonder if I should just build it myself.

<br clear="right" />

---

### 🔭 What I'm working on

* 🔐 Distributed KMS architectures, performance, scaling, and failure behavior
* 🛠️ **Ground Control**, a control plane for heterogeneous infrastructure
* 🐧 Linux, NixOS, reproducible environments, and infrastructure experiments
* 🧠 Local LLMs, AI agents, MCP/LSP tooling, and benchmarking
* 🏠 Self-hosting, homelab projects, automation, and small tools that may or may not have needed to exist
